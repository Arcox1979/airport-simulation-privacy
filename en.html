1. Controller and contact details

Data controller and developer: Antonio Gencarelli.

Privacy and support email: antoniogencarelli@gmail.com.

This policy covers the Android app Airport Control Tower Mayday, package com.towermayday.airportcontrol, and the web page hosting this policy.

2. Game data

The game stores session and career saves, takeoff records for each airport, play time, unlocked airports and content, missions, and preferences such as audio settings and tutorial completion locally on your device. This data lets you resume the game and retain your progress.

No user profile is required, and the app does not send these saves to a server operated by the developer. Device backups and restores depend on the user's Android/Google services and settings.

Game features do not require access to your contacts, camera, microphone or GPS location. Radio messages are game text played through the device's text-to-speech engine, which may use online services depending on its settings; the game does not record your voice.

If you contact the developer by email, your email address, any name you provide, and the contents of your request are processed to reply to you. Do not send passwords or information that is unnecessary for support.

3. Advertising and consent

The app integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) for rewarded ads, for example to unlock content or continue a game. Current internal-testing versions use Google's demonstration ad units.

The advertising service may collect and share with Google your IP address, approximate location inferred from your IP, device or advertising identifiers, interactions with apps and ads, and diagnostic information. This data is used to deliver and measure ads, analyze the service and prevent fraud. SDK data is encrypted in transit.

Processing depends on device settings, service availability and consent choices. The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent where required and check whether ads may be requested. When required by the service, the “Ad privacy” button lets you reopen the options and change your choices. Android settings also let you manage the advertising identifier.

Demonstration ads do not mean that the SDK makes no connections or processes no technical data. More information: Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en) and how Google uses information from apps and sites that use its services (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites?hl=en).

4. Purposes and legal bases

• Providing the game and retaining progress: performance of the service requested by the user, where the data being processed is personal data.

• Responding to support and privacy requests: handling the request, fulfilling applicable obligations and the legitimate interest in providing support.

• Advertising and related identifiers: consent where required by law; Google services also apply the legal bases described in their own policies.

• Security and abuse prevention: the legitimate interest in protecting the service and, where applicable, legal obligations.

Where consent is used, it can be withdrawn without affecting the lawfulness of processing already carried out. Features requiring a rewarded ad depend on ad availability and the applicable permissions.

5. Providers and international transfers

The services involved include Google for advertising and consent management, the email provider for requests sent to the developer, and GitHub for hosting this page. The text-to-speech engine and any backups are device services chosen by the user.

The developer does not sell game saves and does not operate a synchronization service or an online leaderboard. Providers may process data outside the user's country or the European Economic Area. Their policies describe the locations and applicable safeguards, including adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses where relevant. You can request information from the controller using the email address above.

6. Retention and deletion

Progress remains stored on your device until you clear the app's data or remove the app, subject to any system backups. To delete saves on your phone, open Android Settings → Apps → Tower Mayday → Storage → Clear data; the names of these controls may vary. This also deletes local progress and unlocks. Manage any backup copies separately in your device or account settings.

Support communications are retained for as long as necessary to handle the request and, if needed, meet legal obligations or protect rights. Data independently processed by Google and GitHub follows the retention policies of their respective services. Clearing the app's data does not automatically delete data those providers have already processed.

For information or deletion requests, email antoniogencarelli@gmail.com. Because there are no game accounts or copies of saves on the developer's servers, you do not need to request account closure, and the developer cannot remotely delete data held only on your device.

7. Younger users

The intended audience is people aged 13 and over; the game is not intended for children under 13. The game does not ask you to enter your date of birth.

The game's intended age range is not necessarily the same as the age at which someone may independently give privacy consent. Where processing requires consent and the user has not reached the age required by the law of their country, a person with parental responsibility must be involved. In Italy, this threshold is 14 for information society services.

A parent or guardian can contact the controller with concerns about a minor's data.

8. Your rights

Where applicable, you may request access, rectification, deletion, restriction of processing and data portability, object to processing, and withdraw consent. To exercise rights concerning the developer's processing, email the controller, describe your request and do not send more information than necessary.

You may also contact the Italian Data Protection Authority (https://www.garanteprivacy.it/) or your competent supervisory authority. For providers' independent processing, you may also use the tools and contact details in their policies.

GitHub processes technical data needed to deliver and protect the site, including visitors' IP addresses, under its own privacy statement (https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
